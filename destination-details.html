Best Time to Visit

Plan your visit around ideal seasonal weather conditions.

PEAK SEASON
October – March

Cool, pleasant weather with clear skies. Ideal for sightseeing, heritage walks, and hill station treks.

SHOULDER SEASON
April – June

Warm summer months ideal for high-altitude hill escapes (Ooty, Kodaikanal, Munnar) and beach retreats.
